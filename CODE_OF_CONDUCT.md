# Code of Conduct

## Expected behavior

- Treat people working on ADMRN Bridge with respect.
- Keep discussion on the work.
- Assume good faith, and correct mistakes when they are pointed out.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Posting secrets, credentials, account identifiers, or deployment logs.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to hg@admrn.com.

Do not include private personal information in a public issue.

Maintainers may reject or revert contributions that break this code.
